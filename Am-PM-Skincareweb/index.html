The Problem

Why traditional multivitamins are out of sync

Most multivitamins deliver everything in a single pill taken at random hours. But your biology is dynamic throughout the 24-hour cycle.

⏱️

Same Formula Morning + Night

Taking the exact same pill at 8 AM or 10 PM makes zero biological sense. Daytime organs need active focus; nighttime cells need quiet restoration.

⚡

Energy Nutrients At Bedtime

High-dose stimulatory B-complex and metabolism boosters taken in the evening disrupt REM sleep architecture and restful recovery.

😴

Recovery Nutrients During Work

Relaxing minerals and heavy sleep-aid botanicals taken during morning hours induce brain fog, lethargy, and mid-afternoon slumps.

🧩

One-Size-Fits-All Nutrition

Overloaded single pills cause nutrient competition for absorption pathways, meaning most actives pass through unabsorbed.
